Our Story

The OROVAN Philosophy

Leave a lasting impression.

Fragrance is one of the most personal forms of self-expression. It does not ask to be seen; it is remembered.

A scent can recall a place, a person, a version of yourself you had almost forgotten. OROVAN is built around that belief. Each fragrance begins as a study in character — composed to feel considered, intimate, and distinctive rather than loud.

We are taking our time with the collection. The fragrances are still in development, and we would rather reveal them slowly, with care, than rush a signature that is meant to last.

The finest impression is rarely the loudest one.
SVM

The Founder

Shivam Choudhary

Founder of OROVAN

OROVAN is a founder-led fragrance house built on a simple conviction: a scent should feel like an extension of the person wearing it. Shivam Choudhary founded OROVAN to create fragrances that are elegant, expressive, and made to be remembered — with the same care and restraint reflected in every part of the house.
